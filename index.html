BioTailr AI Extension — Setup Guide

BioTailr-AI-Extension.zip Ready

Your download will start automatically. Follow the 4 quick steps below to load it into your browser.

Re-download .ZIP
STEP 01

Extract the ZIP Archive

Unzip the downloaded BioTailr-AI-Extension.zip file into a dedicated folder on your computer (e.g. Documents\BioTailr-AI-Extension).

Keep all files in this folder together so your browser can run the extension.
STEP 02

Open Extensions Page

Open a new browser tab and navigate to your browser's extension manager:

Chrome chrome://extensions
Edge edge://extensions
STEP 03

Enable Developer Mode

In the top-right corner of the Extensions page, switch the Developer mode toggle to ON.

This reveals the "Load unpacked" button required for local installation.
STEP 04

Click 'Load Unpacked' & Pin

Click Load unpacked in the top-left toolbar, then select your unzipped BioTailr-AI-Extension folder.

Click the Extensions puzzle icon in your browser toolbar and pin BioTailr AI for instant access.
